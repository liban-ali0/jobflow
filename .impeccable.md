## Design Context

### Users
Job seekers of any field and level (the author and the friends Jobflow is shared with), often looking abroad; each sets their own roles, levels, and countries. Each runs their own copy locally and opens it most mornings: check what was posted overnight, rank it, pick a few jobs, apply fast. Applying early to fresh postings is the core strategy, so time-sensitivity matters, but the user is often stressed by the search itself.

### Brand Personality
Calm, in control, purposeful. Three words: **steady, clear, departing**. The app should lower stress: everything organised and predictable, with urgency reserved for the moment a fresh job needs action (boarding, last call), never spread across the whole screen.

### Aesthetic Direction
An airport departures hall, and the theme should be pushed further, not diluted: signage top bar, dark departures board with flap-display codes, boarding statuses (Boarding, On time, Last call, Departed), gates and flights as the metaphor for jobs and steps. Signage is high-contrast and legible at a glance, like real wayfinding (yellow/black sign heritage, user-configurable primary and secondary colours).

Around the signature pieces, working surfaces (forms, settings, profile, long text) stay quiet and readable: sentence-case labels, generous spacing, one clear primary action, so the signage stands out rather than everything shouting.

Light and dark mode are both first-class; text colours are derived to meet WCAG AA in each (see `web/src/lib/theme.ts`). Fonts: Barlow Condensed for signage and titles, IBM Plex Sans (plus Plex Sans Arabic) for body, IBM Plex Mono for codes and times.

Anti-reference: corporate HR software (Workday-style): grey, form-heavy, bureaucratic, endless undifferentiated fields.

### Design Principles
1. **Wayfinding over decoration.** Every theme element must help someone find or decide something, as real airport signs do: statuses, codes, and directions carry meaning.
2. **Calm by default, urgent on purpose.** Signal colour and motion are spent only on what is time-sensitive or needs action now.
3. **Signage loud, forms quiet.** The board and top bar carry the identity; inputs and settings are plain, roomy, and easy on the eyes.
4. **Never bureaucratic.** Prefer chips, switches, drag-to-order, and short explanations over walls of labelled text fields.
5. **Readable for everyone.** WCAG AA contrast in both themes, Arabic and right-to-left text handled, keyboard and reduced-motion support.
